We believe consumer tech should fight inflation. Prismo automatically scans and computes fees to keep money in your wallet.

🏢 About Prismo

Founded in 2024 in Mumbai, India, Prismo is a shopping automation platform. Our team builds intelligent APIs that interface with quick-commerce and marketplace apps. By analyzing items client-side, Prismo finds price disparities, calculates shipping costs, platforms fees, surge prices, and executes immediate checkout checkouts — saving you time and money on every single order.

🎯

Our Mission

To save Indian consumers billions of rupees by identifying the lowest price on every single grocery, household, and marketplace item — instantly and automatically.

🔭

Our Vision

A world where purchasing items is frictionless and optimized — where lists are automatically routed, coupon codes applied, and auto-ordered without manual effort.

Our Values
