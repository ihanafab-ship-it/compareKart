🏢 About Prismo

Founded in 2024 in Mumbai, India, Prismo is an AI-powered creative workspace startup. We are a passionate team of engineers, designers, and dreamers on a mission to eliminate repetitive busywork from every team on the planet. Our platform connects 1,000+ tools, learns from your unique workflows, and automates decisions with contextual intelligence — so your team can ship faster, think bigger, and work smarter.
