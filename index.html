✦ Who We Are

A bold mission for every Indian shopper

We believe consumer tech should fight inflation. CompareKart instantly calculates dynamic pricing, hidden packaging charges, and delivery/surge fees to guarantee you always pay the absolute lowest final price.

🏢 About CompareKart

Founded in 2024 in Gurugram, India, CompareKart is a shopping automation platform. Our team builds intelligent APIs that interface with quick-commerce and marketplace apps. By analyzing items client-side, CompareKart finds price disparities, calculates shipping costs, platform fees, surge prices, and executes immediate checkout checkouts — saving you time and money on every single order.

🎯

Our Mission

To save Indian consumers billions of rupees by identifying the lowest price on every single grocery, household, and marketplace item — instantly and automatically.
